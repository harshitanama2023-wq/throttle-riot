How to play

The job

Finish in the top three to unlock the next road and earn prize money. Knock rivals off their bikes for cash and nitro. Grab a rival's weapon by knocking them down. Every crash damages your bike; wreck it and the race is over. If you crash with a cop nearby, you're busted.

Phone and tablet

  • Left pad: steer (slide your thumb across it)
  • Punch: hold to keep swinging at whoever is beside you
  • Kick: slower, but shoves rivals into traffic and off the road
  • Nitro: a burst of speed once the blue bar has charge
  • Throttle is automatic by default; turn it off in Settings to get a Gas button. You can also steer by tilting.

Keyboard

  • ← → or A D steer, ↑/W gas, ↓/S brake
  • Z or J punch, X or K kick, Space nitro, P or Esc pause
